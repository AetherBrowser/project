# Aether Governance

The Aether project is governed by Administrator and Maintainers, who are responsible for technical oversight of the project as laid out in the [**charter**](CHARTER.md). The project follows a simplify decision making model.

The meets in public, and meetings are announced with an issue on this repository. All [meeting minutes are published here](meetings/README.md).

The Administrators or Maintainers can form subcommittees for detailed discussion of issues. Currently there are no active subcommittees.

The Aether project stablishes three levels of collaboration as described below.

## Contributors

People that are collaborating in the project development and have permissions to run try jobs and triage issues adding the correct labels.

### Current Members

Current **Contributors** are listed in [the `CONTRIBUTORS.md` file](CONTRIBUTORS.md).

### Requirements

To be recognized with **Contributor** status, a community member must demonstrate a significant number of nontrivial code contributions which would benefit from access to try jobs, or consistent nontrivial participation in issue reporting and triage.

### Nomination

A **Maintainer** requests the addition of a new **Contributor** in the private *Maintainers* channel in Zulip, listing the number of nontrival contributions by that person. A person may also request that a **Maintainer** nominates them and vouch for them. If no objections are raised within one week, the person is added to the **Contributors** team. Additionally, if four other maintainers express support after at least two days have elapsed then there is no need to wait out the remaining time.

### Removal

People will be removed from this team after 4 months of no activity. A majority of **Maintainers** can also vote to remove a **Contributor**.

## Maintainers

People that can review, approve and merge contributions to the Servo project.

### Current Members

Current **Maintainers** are listed in [the `MAINTAINERS.md` file](MAINTAINERS.md).

### Requirements

To be recognized with **Maintainer** status, a community member must demonstrate expertise in at least one area of Servo (usually via multiple code contributions or technical investigations in a particular crate or subcomponent) and perform several code reviews of nontrivial PRs with an existing reviewer shadowing.

For some of parts of the Aether project, it useful to have a group with maintainer permissions for a subset of our repositories. To add or remove a **Maintainer** in these cases, we'll create a separated team ("repo Maintainers") and follow a similar nomination process.

### Nomination

A **Maintainer** has to nominate a person listing the main highlights of their work and their number of contributions as a *Maintainers* and discussion. Two more **Maintainers** must support the proposal. If no objections are raised within one week, the person is added to the **Maintainers** team.

### Removal

People will be removed from this team after 2 months of no activity. A majority of **Maintainers** can also vote to remove a **Maintainer**.

## Administrators

This is a smaller group of people group of people (maximum 5) that have administrator permissions in the Aether organization and repositories. Administrators are also assigned [ownership](https://github.com/orgs/aetherbrowser/people?query=role%3Aowner) of the Servo GitHub organization.

### Current Members

Current **Administrators** are listed in [the `ADMINISTRATORS.md` file](ADMINISTRATORS.md).

### Requirements

To be assigned **Administrator** status, a community member must have been a **Maintainer Member** for a significant amount of time and have the trust of the Aether community.

### Nomination

If there's an available administrator slot, an **Administrator Member** can nominate a new administrator. The nomination should explain why that person should become **Administrator**. A majority of the **Administrators Members** must approve the addition. If the vote succeeds and no objections are raised within one week, the person is added to the **Administrators** team.

### Removal

Administrators can be removed in several ways:
 - They are automatically removed after 2 months of inactivity.
 - A majority of the **Administrators** can vote to remove an **Administrator**.
 - Any **Administrator** can choose to step down by sending a message to the other Administrators.
